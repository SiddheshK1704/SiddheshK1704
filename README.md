## About

I'm a **CSE (AI & ML)** student at **SRM Institute of Science and Technology**, Chennai. I build at the intersection of **machine learning** and **full-stack software**, and I'd rather understand something from the ground up than skim the surface.

Outside of code, I'm usually deep in **F1 and motorsport engineering**.

- Building: Chrome extensions, RAG systems, and applied ML projects
- Aiming for: production-quality work, not just demos
- Off the clock: F1 strategy, car design, vehicle sketching
